## Beam

Send files between your Windows PC and phones nearby, and mirror your screen to an Apple TV in high resolution. Beam is a fork of MultiDrop 0.2.1.

### New in Beam
- **Redesigned app**: an Apple-style window with a sidebar, grouped settings, and light and dark mode. Nearby devices appear on a radar around your PC; a ring fills as files send. Incoming requests appear as a sheet.
- **Sharper AirPlay mirroring**: choose the picture quality under Screen Mirroring.
  - *High* (default): at least Full HD, 60 fps, about five times MultiDrop's bitrate
  - *Maximum*: up to 4K HEVC on an Apple TV 4K with an NVIDIA graphics card; otherwise the same as High
  - *Standard*: MultiDrop's original settings, for weak Wi-Fi
- Its own name, icon and folders, so it doesn't clash with an installed MultiDrop.

### Install
Run `Beam-Setup.exe`: it installs for your user only, with no admin rights needed. Click **Yes** when Windows asks once, so phones on your Wi-Fi can reach the PC. `Beam.exe` is the portable version (no screen mirroring).

If MultiDrop is running, quit it first: both use the same ports.
